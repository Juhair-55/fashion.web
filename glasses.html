Glasses & Eyewear

Eyewear is more than just a vision aid — it is a fashion statement. From bold frames to minimalist designs, the right pair of glasses can transform your entire look and boost your confidence.

Types of Glasses

Aviator
Classic teardrop-shaped lenses with thin metal frames, originally designed for pilots.
Wayfarer
Bold, angular frames that suit almost every face shape. A timeless design since the 1950s.
Round
Circular lenses that give a vintage, intellectual look.
Blue Light Blocking
Designed to filter blue light from screens, reducing eye strain during long hours of computer use.

How to Choose the Right Frame

  1. Determine your face shape (oval, round, square, or heart)
  2. Choose a frame that contrasts your face shape
  3. Consider your skin tone for frame colour selection
  4. Ensure proper fit around the nose and ears
  5. Try multiple styles before making a decision

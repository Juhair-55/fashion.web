Necklaces & Chains

Necklaces are one of the most versatile accessories in fashion. A well-chosen necklace can elevate a simple outfit, add a touch of elegance, or make a bold fashion statement.

Types of Necklaces

Chain Necklace
Simple linked chains in gold, silver, or platinum. A classic staple for both men and women.
Pendant Necklace
Features a decorative hanging element, often personalised with initials or gemstones.
Choker
Fits closely around the neck, available in velvet, metal, or beaded styles.
Layered Necklace
Multiple chains of different lengths worn together for a trendy, stacked look.

Necklace Length Guide

Standard Necklace Lengths
Style Length Best For
Choker 35-40 cm Casual & Evening wear
Princess 43-48 cm Most versatile, everyday wear
Matinee 50-60 cm Business & Semi-formal
Opera 70-90 cm Formal events

Care Tips

  • Store necklaces separately to prevent tangling
  • Remove jewellery before swimming or showering
  • Clean with a soft cloth regularly
  • Keep away from perfumes and chemicals

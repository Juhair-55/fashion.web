Watches & Timepieces

A watch is more than a time-telling device — it is a symbol of personality and craftsmanship. From sleek minimalist analogs to feature-packed smartwatches, there is a timepiece to match every wrist and lifestyle. The right watch says a lot about who you are without you saying a word.

Watch Movement Types

Quartz
Battery-powered and highly accurate. Low maintenance and affordable. The most common movement type.
Mechanical
Powered by a hand-wound mainspring. Appreciated for its craftsmanship and traditional value.
Automatic
Self-winding mechanical movement powered by the natural motion of the wearer's wrist.
Solar
Powered by light energy, converting it into electricity. Eco-friendly and low maintenance.

How to Pick the Right Watch

  1. Decide the occasion: everyday casual, business formal, or sport
  2. Choose a movement type based on your lifestyle and budget
  3. Select a strap material: leather, stainless steel, or silicone
  4. Check water resistance if you are active or outdoors
  5. Ensure the case diameter matches your wrist size

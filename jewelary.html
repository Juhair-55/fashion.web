Necklaces & Chains

Necklaces are one of the most versatile accessories in fashion. A well-chosen necklace can elevate a simple outfit, add a touch of elegance, or make a bold fashion statement. From dainty gold chains to chunky statement pieces, the world of necklaces has something for every taste.

Types of Necklaces

Chain Necklace
Simple linked chains in gold, silver, or platinum. A classic everyday staple for both men and women.
Pendant Necklace
Features a decorative element hanging from the chain, often personalised with initials or birthstones.
Choker
Sits close to the neck. Available in velvet, metal, lace, or beaded styles. Currently very trendy.
Layered Necklace
Multiple chains of different lengths worn together to create a trendy, stacked effect.
Statement Necklace
Bold, eye-catching pieces designed to be the centrepiece of any outfit. Often worn with simple clothing.

Care & Maintenance Tips

  1. Store necklaces in separate pouches or compartments to prevent tangling
  2. Remove jewellery before swimming, showering, or applying perfume
  3. Clean gently with a soft lint-free cloth after each use
  4. Keep silver pieces in an airtight bag to slow tarnishing
  5. Take precious pieces to a professional jeweller for annual cleaning

Unordered Tips for Buying

  • Check the clasp type: lobster clasp is the most secure
  • Know your metal: sterling silver, gold-plated, or solid gold
  • Consider the outfit necklines you wear most often
  • Read reviews before purchasing online

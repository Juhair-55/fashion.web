About LuxAccessories

LuxAccessories was founded in 2020 with a simple mission: to bring premium, affordable accessories to fashion-forward individuals around the world. Based in Melbourne, Australia, we curate high-quality pieces that elevate any look.

Our Story

What started as a small online boutique has grown into a trusted brand serving thousands of customers across Australia and beyond. We believe accessories are the finishing touch that transforms an outfit from ordinary to extraordinary.

What We Offer

  • Handcrafted jewellery made from sustainable materials
  • Premium leather watches with Swiss movements
  • Designer sunglasses with UV400 protection
  • Luxury handbags and wallets
  • Seasonal collections updated quarterly

Glasses & Eyewear

Eyewear is more than just a vision aid — it is a fashion statement. From bold frames to minimalist designs, the right pair of glasses can transform your entire look and boost your confidence. Whether you need prescription lenses or just want to rock a stylish pair, there is something for everyone.

Types of Glasses

Aviator
Classic teardrop-shaped lenses with thin metal frames, originally designed for pilots in the 1930s. Suits oval and heart-shaped faces best.
Wayfarer
Bold, angular frames popularised by Ray-Ban in the 1950s. A timeless design that suits almost every face shape.
Round
Circular lenses that give a vintage, intellectual look. Great for square and oblong face shapes.
Cat-Eye
Upswept outer edges that give a retro feminine look. Best for round and heart-shaped faces.
Blue Light Blocking
Designed to filter harmful blue light from digital screens, reducing eye strain during long hours of computer use.

How to Choose the Right Frame

  1. Determine your face shape: oval, round, square, heart, or oblong
  2. Choose a frame shape that contrasts your face shape for balance
  3. Consider your skin tone when selecting frame colour
  4. Try multiple styles and sizes before committing
  5. Ensure the nose bridge and temples fit comfortably
